👤 About Us

ARNAUD AFROCHAINE VERIFICATION LTD is a CAC registered company based in Sango Ota, Ogun State, Nigeria. We are founded to fight the epidemic of fake certificates in Nigeria.

We don't issue certificates. We VERIFY and AUTHENTICATE certificates issued by schools, WAEC, NECO, Universities and training centers. Our system uses QR Code + Firebase technology to make verification instant and fraud-proof.

Our Mission: To restore trust in Nigerian education system and help employers verify credentials in 5 seconds.

Founder: Arnaud Afrochaine Team - Education Technology Experts

🛠️ Our Services

  • Certificate Verification: Verify WAEC, NECO, School Results, University Degrees with QR Code
  • School Result Management: We help schools upload student results to secure Firebase portal
  • Instant QR Generation: Each certificate gets unique Black QR that links to verification portal
  • Employer Verification Portal: Companies can verify job applicants instantly without calling school
  • Fake Certificate Detection: Our system flags invalid or tampered certificates as NOT FOUND
  • Digital Archiving: Secure cloud storage for all academic records
