👤 About Us

ARNAUD AFROCHAINE VERIFICATION LTD is a CAC registered verification company based in Sango Ota, Ogun State. We fight fake certificates in Nigeria.

We don't issue certificates. We VERIFY and AUTHENTICATE certificates from schools, WAEC, NECO, Universities using QR Code + Firebase technology.

Mission: Restore trust in Nigerian education, help employers verify credentials in 5 seconds.
